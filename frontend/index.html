What is Haramball?

A simple idea, a serious mission.

Haramball is a term football fans use for the worst kind of football — cynical, time-wasting, defensive, soulless. The football that makes you check your phone instead of the pitch.

This site lets you vote for the team you think is the biggest haramball offender right now. One vote per day. Pick a reason — time-wasting, parking the bus, diving — and optionally add a short comment. Your votes feed the weekly, monthly, yearly, and all-time leaderboards.

No registration, no accounts, no paywalls.

New to the term? Read our full guide: What is Haramball?

Tag @haramball11 on socials when your team gets robbed by haramball football. We feature the worst clips of the week.

How voting works One vote per device per day. Votes are timestamped (UTC) so the weekly leaderboard rolls over Monday, monthly on the 1st, yearly on January 1.
Coverage Currently — top-flight clubs across — leagues worldwide, spanning all six confederations (UEFA, CONMEBOL, CONCACAF, AFC, CAF, OFC). Missing your team? Send us a message.
